# Punch Up! game design

## Hook

Write the line. Win the room. Each prompt is a tiny comedy setup presented like a marked-up screenplay. Two anonymous punchlines face off at a time, and the rest of the room picks the line that deserves to stay in the script.

## Player loop

1. Read two private assignments on your controller.
2. Write one punchline for each assignment.
3. Wait while the writers’ room finishes.
4. Vote in each matchup you did not write.
5. Watch the winning line and score reveal on the shared display.

## Rounds and scoring

- Round 1 — First draft: 100 points per vote.
- Round 2 — Rewrite: 200 points per vote.
- Round 3 — The big punch: every player answers one shared finale prompt, then votes for another writer’s line, for 300 points per vote.
- A line that receives every eligible vote earns a 250 × round-multiplier “Nailed it” bonus.
- The highest score after the finale wins. Ties share the win.

## Timing

- Instructions: 8 seconds.
- Writing: 70 seconds in rounds 1–2; 55 seconds in the finale.
- Voting: 22 seconds per duel; the all-player finale gets 30 seconds for 3–5 players and 35 seconds for 6–8 players.
- Results: 9 seconds per matchup.
- Round break: 6 seconds.

Phases advance when everyone finishes or when time expires. The host display never asks for input.

## Content principles

Prompts are specific, safe for mixed groups, and short enough to parse at a glance. They invite surprising lines without requiring trivia, impersonation, or personal disclosure.
