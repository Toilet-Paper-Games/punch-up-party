# Product

## Players

Groups of 3–8 people sharing a television or large browser display, with one phone controller per player.

## Promise

Punch Up! turns the room into a writers’ room: write the missing line, vote on the sharpest punchline, and watch the winning script take over the screen.

## Product rules

- The shared display is always passive.
- Every gameplay action happens on a controller.
- The game teaches itself through the current controller task.
- A full session lasts about 12 minutes across two duel rounds and one all-player finale.
- Reconnecting players recover submitted answers and votes from durable player state.
